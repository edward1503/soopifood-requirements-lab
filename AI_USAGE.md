The student used ChatGPT/Codex to read the tutorial, draft interview questions, simulate research, synthesise personas, review source support, propose a text storyboard, simulate walkthroughs, draft and revise requirements, construct a backlog, prioritise scope, write acceptance criteria and audit the supplied PR fixture. The assistant also prepared the README, checked document consistency and packaged the Git repository at the student's request.

All generated interviews, observations, walkthroughs and stakeholder clarification answers are explicitly simulated. Persona names are composite labels. No real participants, fieldwork or stakeholder/privacy approval are asserted. Numeric targets introduced in clarification are provisional assumptions, not empirical measurements.

Review files record assistant-performed critiques. They do not certify independent student or teammate review. Work continued in one assistant conversation; fresh independent sessions were not created per part. For F/H, first-round files were saved before the stakeholder notes and promo rules were read in the completion turn. Hashes preserve those files but do not establish an independently blinded experiment.

The prototype is textual. The Gherkin parser checks syntax; an arithmetic oracle checks illustrative expectations. There is no SoopiFood implementation, step-definition suite, performance run, usability study or security/compliance certification. The DoD audit concerns a supplied mock PR and does not claim a live pipeline run or code repair.

The provided tutorial, prompts, inputs and samples are course materials; their authorship is not claimed. Samples were consulted after the assistant's drafts/reviews, with differences recorded in `workspace/sample_comparison.md`. This disclosure describes performed work and is not an official institutional disclosure form or instructor approval.
