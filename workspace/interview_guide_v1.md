# SoopiFood: Restaurant Manager Interview Guide — Draft 1

**Duration:** 45–60 minutes; suggested timings below total 55 minutes.

**Purpose:** Understand the manager's current work, experiences and needs during service, before making design decisions for SoopiFood.

**Interviewer notes:** Ask the main questions in order, but follow the manager's account naturally. Use the probes selectively, rather than treating them as extra questions that must all be asked. Ask for recent examples, use the manager's own terms, and leave room for positive, negative or mixed experiences. Do not assume the restaurant already uses a tablet or delivery platform. Keep the discussion within the manager's day-to-day responsibilities.

## Warm-up — 5 minutes

Briefly explain the purpose of the conversation and that there are no right or wrong answers. Let the manager know they can skip any question.

**1. Tell me about your role during a typical service period.**

- Which activities are you responsible for personally?
- How are responsibilities shared with other people on the shift?

## Current workflow — 12 minutes

**2. Think of a recent order during service. Talk me through what happened from the restaurant first receiving it until the food left the restaurant.**

- How did you find out about the order and decide what to do next?
- How did you keep track of its progress?

**3. What tools or information do you use to manage orders during a shift?**

- Walk me through a recent example of how you used them.
- What do you do when information is missing or unclear, if that happens?

## Pain points — 10 minutes

**4. How does the way you manage orders vary across a shift?**

- When does the process work as you expect?
- In what situations, if any, does it need extra attention from you?

**5. Tell me about a recent order or service period that stood out to you.**

- How did what happened compare with what you expected?
- What effect did it have on your work or on the people involved?

## Coordination with riders and customers — 12 minutes

**6. How are pickups coordinated when a delivery rider is involved?**

- What information passes between the restaurant and the rider before handover?
- When timing or arrangements change, if that happens, how is it handled?

**7. What contact, if any, do you have with customers about their orders during service?**

- Walk me through a recent example.
- How do you decide what to handle yourself and what to pass to someone else?

## Success and priorities — 11 minutes

**8. What tells you that a service period has gone well?**

- What signs or information do you look at?
- How do the views of your team, customers or riders inform that judgement?

**9. Looking at how orders are managed today, what would you want to keep or change?**

- What makes those things important to your day-to-day work?
- If you have several priorities, how would you decide which matters most?

## Wrap-up — 5 minutes

**10. What else should we understand about managing orders here?**

- What have we missed or misunderstood in this conversation?
- Whose perspective would help us understand the work further, and why?

Briefly summarise what you heard in the manager's own terms and invite corrections. Thank them for their time.

**If time runs short:** Cut Q5 first if earlier answers already include a detailed example. Cut Q3 next if tools and information were covered while walking through Q2. Use fewer probes before dropping other main questions. Keep Q9 and Q10 so the manager can state their priorities and correct omissions.
