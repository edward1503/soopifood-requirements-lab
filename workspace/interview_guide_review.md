**AI-assisted tutorial review.** This record was added during completion of the submission to document the existing `interview_guide_v1.md` → `interview_guide_v2.md` revision. It does not claim a separate human review.

| Question | Review of version 1 | Treatment in version 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Open invitation about responsibilities; no assumed distress. | Retained. |
| Q2 | Assumes the order ultimately leaves the restaurant and the manager tracks progress. | Allows any eventual outcome; asks whether progress was followed. |
| Q3 | Does not prescribe digital tools; missing-information probe is conditional. | Retained. |

| Q9 | Allows retaining existing practices as well as change. | Retained. |
| Q10 | Probes assume omissions and another useful perspective. | Makes additions, corrections and other perspectives optional. |

The supplied iteration prompt's examples about “juggle” and “most frustrating challenges” were absent from our draft; its neutrality principle was applied to actual wording. Version 2 retains six sections, ten main questions, two optional probes per question, a 55-minute schedule and cut-first guidance. Neutral wording reduces leading assumptions; it does not establish that a real participant would interpret every question identically.
